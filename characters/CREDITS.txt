JESSE (jesse.v2.glb): the character in the Speciale45 configurator's outro

Jesse's likeness, his two photographs and what was made from them (the face
and baked skin textures, the embroidery and slipper emblem details) belong to
Jesse and the owner of Speciale45. They are not CC0 or Apache-licensed and may
not be reused.

The character was built for Speciale45 from these open sources:

Body base mesh and skeleton
  "Male Base Mesh - 3D Person (Rigged, A-Pose)" by Innerscene, adapted from
  MakeHuman. CC0 1.0 Universal. See MAKEHUMAN-LICENSE.txt.
  https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/male-base-mesh-3d-person-rigged-a-pose-380e6d89
  Rescaled, limb rest positions changed, the head replaced.

Anatomical head (cranium, ears, neck, jaw underside)
  Blender Studio and Blender Community, Human Base Meshes v1.4.1. CC0 1.0.
  See BLENDER-BASE-MESHES-LICENSE.txt.
  https://download.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/
  Reshaped and joined to the fitted face.

Face topology
  MediaPipe (Google) canonical face topology. Apache License 2.0.
  See MEDIAPIPE-LICENSE.txt. https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
  Modified: adapted, fitted to the reference, extended into the forehead and
  cranium, given photographic UV coordinates, decimated for the web.

Clothing, slippers, bracelet, hair, the fabric normal texture and the three
animations (walk, idle, nice_choice) were made for this project.
